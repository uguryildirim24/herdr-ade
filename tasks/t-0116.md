## What to do

A login row must ask the question that matches how the model is actually
reached. Where a model runs through pi's provider, the provider's own readiness
is the answer — `herdr-pi check <provider>` already gives it, and the doctor
already calls it. A standalone binary's login is only the right question when a
recipe actually starts that binary.

Derive it from the recipe, the way D12 derived placement readiness: a `kind =
"pi"` recipe's readiness comes from its provider; a native recipe's from its own
binary. Do not keep a second, name-based list of which logins to probe.
