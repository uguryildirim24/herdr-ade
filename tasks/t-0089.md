## U2 — a verb for overturning a decision

`ha decide "<line>" --class routine|what-you-get|money|undo` records a choice the
coordinator made without asking, and the project screen shows those under
"Decided for you". Rolf can see them and cannot act on them: reversing one today
is a sentence he types into chat and hopes the coordinator honours. The record
exists, the action does not.

Add the action. A decision can be overturned by its id, with a reason. The
overturn is itself recorded — the original decision stays in the log, marked
overturned, with who overturned it, when, and why; nothing is deleted. The
project screen shows an overturned decision as overturned rather than dropping
it, and the coordinator's context read surfaces a fresh overturn so the next
turn acts on it instead of repeating the decision.

Decide the verb's shape yourself and say why in your report. An overturn Rolf
types must be one short command.

## U3 — an ask can be withdrawn, and a duplicate is refused

Tonight the same question was put on Rolf's board twice (a-12 and a-13), because
the first `ha ask` was read through `tail` and its id never seen. Only the newest
open ask may be `--reask`ed, so the older duplicate could not be corrected and is
still sitting on his board, a card both of us know is wrong.

Two parts:

- **Withdraw.** An open ask can be withdrawn by id, with a reason. It leaves the
  board and stays in the record as withdrawn. Withdrawing an answered ask is
  refused with a reason.
- **Refuse a duplicate at write time.** `ha ask` refuses a question that is
  materially the same as an open one and names the existing id instead of
  creating a second card. Match on the question text with the plain-word
  normalisation the codebase already has; do not invent a similarity model, and
  do not call out to anything. An exact repeat after normalisation is enough —
  say in your report what you matched on and what it will miss.

While you are here: `ha ask` should print the new id as the first thing on the
line, so a `tail` cannot hide it.
