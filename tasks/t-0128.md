# Rounds stop waiting for each other (D15)

## The problem

A round that is under review owns its integration branch. `round open` and
`round review` on the same branch are refused with `round_head_reserved`
(`require_branch_available`, `src/round.rs` around line 503). Every project
integrates into `main`, so one round in review blocks every other round in
every project that shares the repository, until it merges or is abandoned.

Reported from flyonenomics and seen here: while r56 was in review, r57's lane
had finished and could not even be put into a round. A round's review is often
the slowest step (a cold reviewer start on the box takes minutes), so the whole
pipeline runs one round at a time. Rolf wants this fixed.

## What to build

1. Opening, admitting and reviewing a round never wait on another round. Only
   the merge transaction itself is serialized on the branch.
2. A round whose review was built on a base that has since moved does not
   fail at merge time and wait for the coordinator. When `round merge` finds
   that the branch head moved since the verdict, the harness makes the next
   review revision and starts the repair reviewer on its own (the same path a
   `merge_conflict` takes today via `round review` + `round advance`), and the
   round record says so in one line. If the new base merges cleanly with no
   conflict, decide whether a repair review is needed at all and say what you
   decided and why.
3. Remove the reservation and everything that exists only to support it: the
   refusal, its hint text, tests that assert it, and the sentences in the
   coordinator and reviewer skill text that tell anyone to finish or abandon a
   round before opening another. Keep `round abandon` itself; it has other uses.
4. Two rounds reviewing at once on the same repository must not step on each
   other's review branches, worktrees or locks. Check the lock order in
   `round.rs` (repository lock, then project lock) still holds.

## Rules that apply

From RULES.md: remove, do not guard. No shims, no compatibility path for the
old behaviour, no flag to turn the reservation back on. Historical round
records must still load. Do not add a test unless it fails on a real defect
this brief names; a test that two rounds can open and review on one branch at
the same time, and that a moved head triggers the repair review, qualifies.
