A lane's DONE line never reached the coordinator's pane, although its inbox item was written. Rolf noticed (2026-09-19 ~23:45Z): "task 42 is done. Why did you not get a notification?" and then "if you can see your inbox, then why are you getting text messages into your pane? Is that not redundant?" The answer we want to keep true: the typed line is the wake-up (the coordinator only reads its inbox when it is already awake); the inbox item is the record. So a done event must always be typed, once, and never silently skipped.

Evidence in `/Users/rolfie/.herdr-ade/adeherdr`:

- `deliveries/t-0048-1-2.jsonl` holds one line, `{"state":"acknowledged"}`, and no `submitted`; `receipts/t-0048-1-2.toml` created 22:06:31Z; the inbox got `20260919T220641Z-round-advance-r21-80` (the hook's `ha round advance` announcing the verdict) and `20260919T220649Z-report-available-t-0048-81`.
- `deliveries/t-0047-1-2.jsonl` (a delivery that worked minutes earlier) holds `submitted` then `acknowledged`.

`steps.rs::deliver_events` (`src/steps.rs:59`) skips any event whose journal has a line, so an `acknowledged` written before the ticker typed the line means the line is never typed. Find who appended `Acknowledged` for t-0048-1-2 without a `Submitted` (start at every `append_delivery(.., Acknowledged)` call; the suspect is the path the herdr hook runs on a lane state change, `ha round advance`, or the receipt/announce path that runs when the harness consumes a reviewer's verdict).

Rules to implement:

1. `acknowledged` means the coordinator itself saw the line. Only commands the coordinator runs (`ha context`, `ha inbox done`, `ha round show`, `ha thread show <id>`, whichever already acknowledge today) may write it. Automation (the ticker, the courier, `round advance` run by the hook, `say`) never acknowledges a delivery.
2. An event whose journal has `acknowledged` or `handled` but no `submitted` is a defect the ticker repairs: it types the line and appends `submitted` (keeping the later states), so the wake-up still happens. One line in the inbox item text or the doctor is not needed; the journal tells the story.
3. Keep the rest: typed only into the coordinator's ready pane, once per event, `recipient-changed` on a replaced coordinator, nothing typed for a superseded lane attempt.
4. `docs/operations.md` (deliveries section): two sentences, the wake-up line versus the inbox record, and who may acknowledge.

Tests with the fake runner and the events testkit: automation consuming a verdict leaves the journal without `acknowledged`; a journal with `acknowledged` and no `submitted` gets typed once and then `submitted`; a normal event still types once and is not retyped after `submitted`.
